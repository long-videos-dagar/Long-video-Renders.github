The zombie apocalypse reached the city on a Tuesday. Very, very slowly.

At sunrise, a jogger spotted one zombie, a mile outside downtown. The mayor sounded the alarm. The city boarded up every window, emptied every shelf, and waited.

By noon, the zombie had reached the bus stop. By sunset, it was still waiting for the bus.

On day two, the city got bored. The evening news started taking bets. Someone opened a coffee cart, right on the zombie's route. The zombie bought a latte.

On day three, it finally reached City Hall. The whole police force took aim. The zombie groaned, raised a rotting hand, and bit the mayor. Gently. It had no teeth.

The doctor took one look, and lowered his glasses. It wasn't a zombie. It was Grandpa Walt. He'd walked all the way from the retirement home, to complain about the Wi-Fi.
